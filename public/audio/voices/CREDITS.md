# Voice credits

The crew's lines are spoken by synthetic voices from ElevenLabs text to speech (model eleven_multilingual_v2), from the words in tools/voice-lines.json.
Whether commercial use is allowed depends on the ElevenLabs plan the lines were made under: check https://elevenlabs.io/terms before shipping.

- **Lookout (masthead telephone)**: the premade voice "Daniel" over a telephone filter. 15 lines.
- **Gunnery officer (voice-pipe)**: the premade voice "George" over a pipe filter. 19 lines.
- **Damage control (voice-pipe)**: the premade voice "Alice" over a pipe filter. 12 lines.
- **Wireless office (aircraft and spotters)**: the premade voice "Lily" over a wireless filter. 6 lines.
